A video player for one side, a command line for the other.

Point at a frame. Your agent reads it as JSON.

Havooch is a native macOS video player for giving feedback to coding agents. You pause, draw a region and write a note. ⌘↩ sends the queue down the wire, and the agent that is listening in your shell gets the time, the keyframe, the crop and the transcript. It works in your repo and replies on the frame.

Install for Mac See the whole loop

Free and open source, MIT. macOS 26. The agent side is one skill that Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or any agent with a shell can run.

Both ends of the wire

Your side is a player. The agent's side is a shell. Every step on the left has one command on the right, and nothing in between needs a server.

Pause and point

Space pauses. Drag on the frame to mark a region. The time becomes the thread's time.

havooch wait

Comment and queue

Write what you mean. ↩ queues it. Keep watching and keep queueing; each frame gets its own thread.

Nothing yet. The queue is yours until you send it.

⌘↩ sends the queue

One send, grouped by thread. The time, the keyframe, the region crop, the transcript around it and your context for the video.

havooch wait prints the send as JSONhavooch ack s-… "Got 3 messages, starting."

Your messages turn acknowledged in the player.

Pins on the timeline show each thread's state while you keep watching.

havooch status m-… working

The agent reads the keyframe and the crop, edits your repo and commits once per message.

A question lands on the frame

The thread moves to Needs you. Your answer skips the queue and goes straight back.

havooch ask 3 "Cmd+Return or Cmd+Enter?" waits, then prints your answer

The answer, beside the frame

One to three plain sentences and the commit, in a thread you can reopen from the pin.

The send, as the agent reads it

One JSON document per ⌘↩. Threads are in time order, General first. Paths are absolute, so the agent can open the keyframe and the crop with the tools it already has.

  • threads[].time is the exact second you paused on.
  • keyframePath is that frame as a PNG; cropPath is the region you drew, cut out.
  • transcript is the fifteen seconds around the moment, when the video has one.
  • history carries the earlier messages on the thread, so a follow-up has its past.
  • context is your note about the subject: a project, a design, a setup. Set it in the app or in a sidecar file beside the video.

The command line

The app ships a havooch command. The listener uses five of its verbs and never touches the player. Everything else drives the player under a lease, so an agent can review a video with you and give the controls back the moment you want them.

For the listener

wait [--timeout s]
Wait for the next send and print it as JSON.
ack <send> [text]
Say you have it. The messages turn acknowledged.
status <message> working|done|failed
Say how far you are. The pin shows it.
reply <thread> <text>
Answer on a thread, by id or by number.
ask <thread> <question>
Ask on the frame and print the person's answer.

For driving the player

control take control release
Take the lease, or hand it back.
player open <path> player seek 0:12
Open a video, move to a time, play, pause.
comment add <text> --at 12 --region x,y,w,h
Queue a comment where a person would.
thread show 3 thread list
Open a thread in the sidebar, or go back to the list.
screenshot out.png --appearance dark
Capture the window as it is.

The lease is the whole safety story: one holder at a time, you can take it back from the toolbar, and a listener never needs it. Exit codes are plain: 0 done, 1 refused with the reason on stderr, 2 a wait ran out.

Install

Either line installs the app, the havooch command and the havooch-mate skill.

Homebrew brew install --cask yahyabedirhan/tap/havooch
Script curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yahyabedirhan/havooch/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

The script checks the release's SHA-256 before it installs; --uninstall removes everything. Havooch is signed ad hoc, not notarized, so on first launch macOS asks once: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. Then tell your agent to listen for Havooch feedback and open the demo from the empty window.
