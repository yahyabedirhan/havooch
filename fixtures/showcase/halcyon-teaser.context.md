# Context: halcyon-teaser

## Topic

The launch teaser for Halcyon, a made-up weather app that tells you how the day will feel in plain words instead of numbers.

## Goal

A 40-second teaser for the launch page and social posts: a warm, calm first look at the app, with a cold open, the logo, the today screen, a rain alert, the week and an end card.

## Source repos

- The teaser's scenes live in the local explainer studio (Remotion and Kokoro), as the video `halcyon-teaser`.

## Note

The voice and the colour are close to final. Review the pacing, the type and every on-screen time.
