# Context: lantern-teaser

## Topic

The launch teaser for Lantern, a made-up developer tool that traces how one request moves through your services and shows where the time goes.

## Goal

A 40-second teaser for the launch page and social posts: a calm first look at the tool, with a cold open on one slow request, the logo, the service map, the trace waterfall, the fix and an end card.

## Source repos

- The teaser's scenes live in the local explainer studio (Remotion and Kokoro), as the video `lantern-teaser`.

## Note

The voice and the Graphite look are close to final. Review the pacing, the diagrams, the type and every number on screen.
